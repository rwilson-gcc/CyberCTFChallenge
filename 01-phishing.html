What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals impersonate legitimate organisations via email, SMS, or fake websites to trick victims into revealing sensitive information — passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

The name comes from "fishing" — attackers cast a wide net (mass emails) hoping someone takes the bait. Spear phishing is targeted at a specific individual using personal details.

📧

Email Phishing

Bulk emails impersonating banks, HMRC, or services like PayPal or Amazon to steal logins.

🎯

Spear Phishing

Targeted attacks using personal info (name, job role) to appear highly convincing.

📱

Smishing

SMS phishing — fake texts claiming to be parcel delivery, banks, or HMRC.

📞

Vishing

Voice phishing — phone calls from "tech support" or "your bank" creating urgency.

Common Red Flags

🚩

Urgency

"Act now or your account will be suspended!" Pressure tactics bypass rational thinking.

🚩

Suspicious Sender

support@paypa1-secure.com vs support@paypal.com — look carefully at the domain.

🚩

Mismatched Links

Hover over links before clicking. The display text may say one thing; the URL another.

🚩

Generic Greetings

"Dear Valued Customer" — legitimate companies usually use your actual name.

⚠ Remember: No legitimate bank, government body, or major company will ever ask for your password, PIN, or full card number via email.
