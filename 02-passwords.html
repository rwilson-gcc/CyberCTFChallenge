How Passwords Get Cracked

Attackers use several techniques to compromise passwords. Understanding these methods helps you build better defences.

🔨 Brute Force

Try every possible combination. Short passwords (under 8 chars) can be cracked in seconds with modern hardware.

📖 Dictionary Attack

Try common words, phrases, and leaked passwords. "password123" appears in billions of breach databases.

🌈 Rainbow Tables

Pre-computed hash lookups. Salting passwords defeats this by adding randomness before hashing.

🧠 Credential Stuffing

Using breached username/password pairs from one site to log into others. Reusing passwords is catastrophic.

What Makes a Strong Password?

Length is the single most important factor. A 16-character passphrase is far stronger than an 8-character "complex" password.
